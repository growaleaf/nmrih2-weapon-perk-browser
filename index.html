No More Room in Hell 2 — Weapon & Perk Browser

A reference for No More Room in Hell 2's guns, melee weapons, and level-up perk pool. Every tier and every number below is labeled by how many independent sources confirm it — a single-sourced figure is shown as single-sourced, not silently treated as settled. This page does not hand you a verdict; it hands you what the sources actually say, including where they disagree with each other. Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere — it renders from the data in this page in your browser.
